FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does a Product Marketing Manager do?

A Product Marketing Manager bridges product, sales, and marketing: shaping positioning and messaging, leading go-to-market strategy for new launches, enabling sales teams, and using customer and market research to guide what gets built and how it's sold.

Why hire a Product Marketing Manager based in Spain for a European market?

A Spain-based PMM working across Europe brings on-the-ground understanding of regional buyer behavior and language nuance, while operating in convenient time zones for teams across the EU and UK. It combines local market fluency with pan-European GTM experience.

What industries does Laura specialize in as a Product Marketing Manager?

Cloud and cybersecurity, fintech and Web3, marketplaces, and AI and emerging tech. Each of these brings different buyer complexity, from technical procurement cycles to fast-moving consumer trust dynamics.

How does Laura use AI in product marketing?

AI is built into the workflow, not bolted on: AI-powered lead generation systems, automated competitive intelligence and battle cards, and prompt-driven research tools that speed up positioning and messaging work without losing quality control.
